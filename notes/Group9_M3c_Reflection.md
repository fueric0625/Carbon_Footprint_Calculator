# Milestone 3c — Exit ticket (individual reflection)

Canvas 文本框提交，100–150 词。两个问题各答一句到两句，用自己的话。

## 正文（可直接粘贴）

Revisit 2 forced me to admit a unit error I had made myself. I wrote the headline as kg CO₂e, but the two factors I could source, Shanghai electricity and China high-speed rail, are published as CO₂, while food and travel factors were provisional figures. Prototype 1's own factor table showed it: two rows marked sourced, seven marked "not yet verified", and one headline unit above all of them.

With one group member, the challenge came from re-reading Finding 2 against the running page, not from discussion. That changed my decision. I had planned to defend 783.7 kg as the result. Instead I split it into 96.1 kg CO₂ with a source and 687.6 kg CO₂e without, and marked the meal advice illustrative. The number did not move; what changed is that it can no longer be read as a verified ranking.
