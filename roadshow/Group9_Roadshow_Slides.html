Why · Lectures 1 and 2

Two things he needs from the result

NEED 01

A semester, not only a total

Lecture 1 set 3.84 and 2.54 t CO₂e per student per year side by side. One counts on-campus operations, the other consumption-side spending. They cannot be ranked until the unit and the boundary match.

Sanity check: two 18-week semesters is about 1.6 t — below both.

NEED 02

A comparison he is allowed to trust

Electricity and high-speed rail have published CO₂ factors. Food, metro, and ride-hailing do not. A large unverified number must not outrank a sourced one.

Both needs point the same way: a sourced number and an unverified one never share a headline.

Detail · Units and gases

One number, two units named

CO₂e = Σ (gas × GWP100)

A CO₂e total weights each gas by its 100-year warming potential. CO₂ and CO₂e are different baskets, so this page names them apart.

SOURCED CO₂

687.6 kg

Both sourced rows are published as CO₂ only. Adding the other combustion gases would raise them, so 96.1 kg sits on the low side of a CO₂e.

So the headline says reported and names both parts. It is not a compliant CO₂e total.
